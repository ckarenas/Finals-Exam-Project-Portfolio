### 📄 Activity 07 — Comparing AI-Generated Nursing Résumés

**Short description**

I gave two AI models the same prompt and compared their résumé drafts for structure, clarity, formatting, and overall quality. I used the comparison to write a one-page résumé for an entry-level staff nurse position after graduation and licensure.

**What have I learned?**

- I learned to judge each draft by how easily a reader could find my clinical experience and understand what I did during duty.
- My biggest challenge was fitting my rotations and leadership experience on one page. I grouped my duties by rotation and removed skills that I had already described.
- Specific examples made my roles clearer. I assigned duties as student head nurse, coordinated ward activities as student charge nurse, and used the phrase “under supervision” when describing medication administration and IV insertion.
- For future applications, I can tailor my résumé to the position by choosing the most relevant clinical examples and checking each detail against my CV.
