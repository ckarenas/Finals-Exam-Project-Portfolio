### 🌐 Activity 06 — Creating My One-Page Nursing Portfolio Website

**Short description**

I created a one-page website to introduce myself as a fourth-year nursing student at Ateneo de Davao University. The portfolio shows my classmates and clinical instructors what I learned during supervised clinical rotations and introduces our community research on health literacy and medication adherence among adults with type 2 diabetes.

**What have I learned?**

- I learned to write a clear prompt for a website builder by naming the sections I wanted and explaining who would read them.
- My main challenge was deciding which clinical experiences to include on one page. I chose one example from each rotation, such as how listening to a patient’s main concern helped me during OB triage.
- After building the page, I used the section headings in my prompt as a checklist to make sure the website covered my introduction, clinical experience, and research.
- In the future, I can update the portfolio after each rotation with a short example of a skill I practiced and what I learned from it.
