### 🩺 Activity 05 — Improving an AI Prompt for Nursing Education

**Short description**

I compared two ChatGPT responses about preventing pressure injuries in hospitalized patients. I started with the question “How do nurses prevent pressure injuries?” and then revised it to ask for practical examples, reasons for each intervention, and a checklist that a nursing student could review before clinical duty.

**What have I learned?**

- I learned that naming my audience and explaining what I need helps me write a clearer prompt.
- My first prompt gave me a list of nursing actions, but I still wanted to understand why each action mattered.
- To solve this, I asked ChatGPT to organize the answer into five areas: risk assessment, repositioning and mobility, skin care, nutrition and hydration, and patient education.
- Comparing the answers taught me that examples and rationales make a topic easier to understand, while accuracy still needs a separate check.
- In the future, I can use this approach to create study guides for other nursing topics and check the content against current guidelines and hospital policy before clinical duty.
